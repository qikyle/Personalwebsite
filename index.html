About
I grew up in Davis, California — a proper cow town — and arrived at UC Berkeley curious about everything: economics, data, teaching. I left knowing I wanted to be close to where businesses are built and broken. A consulting role gave me a rare seat inside a Private Equity practice — running commercial due diligences for mega-funds, then a secondment as Chief of Staff at a health-tech startup where I helped close a $55M Series A. From there, growth equity at Serent Capital: lower-middle-market SaaS, hands-on operations, and a platform deal I sourced and closed. Now at Llama Ventures I back Pre-Seed and Seed founders building AI-native companies — leading technical diligences, sitting on boards, and asking the same question every time: what’s actually defensible?
