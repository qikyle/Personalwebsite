03 / Teaching & Giving Back

The work I do
for free is the work
I care about most.

My minor in Education at Berkeley wasn't a box to check — it reflected a genuine belief that teaching is one of the most high-leverage things a person can do.

Since February 2022 I've been a Board Member and Teacher Volunteer at the Chinatown Community Children's Center in San Francisco, working with preschool-age children from low-income families.

In venture I look for founder conviction — the kind that shows up before there's a paycheck attached. Teaching is mine.
